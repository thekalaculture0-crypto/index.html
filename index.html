QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How painful is the tattooing process? ▼
Pain levels vary depending on the tattoo's size, placement, and your personal tolerance. Our professional artists work carefully to make the experience as comfortable as possible. Most clients describe it as pressure rather than pain.
How much does a tattoo cost at The Kala Culture Tattoos? ▼
Tattoo pricing depends on the size, design, placement, and detailing required. Our starting price is ₹399 per sq. inch, and special offers up to 30% OFF may be available. Contact us for an exact quote based on your design.
Can you cover up my old or unwanted tattoo? ▼
Yes! We offer custom tattoo cover-ups. Our artist will assess your existing tattoo and create a design that effectively incorporates or conceals it. Cover-ups require careful planning and design.
How do I properly care for my new tattoo? ▼
Follow the aftercare instructions provided by our artist. Keep the tattoo clean, avoid unnecessary touching or scratching, and follow the recommended moisturizing and healing routine. Proper aftercare ensures vibrant results.
Do I need to book an appointment before getting a tattoo? ▼
Appointments are recommended, especially for larger or customized tattoos. You can contact The Kala Culture Tattoos on WhatsApp or call us to discuss your idea and reserve your slot. We're open 11AM-10PM daily.
How do I choose the right tattoo design and placement? ▼
You don't have to decide everything yourself. Our artist can discuss your idea, preferred style, size, and placement with you and help create a custom design that suits your vision perfectly.
