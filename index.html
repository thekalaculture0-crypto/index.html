Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know before getting my first tattoo?

Discuss your design ideas with our expert artists, understand the process, and follow the aftercare instructions provided by our team.

What are the aftercare instructions for a new tattoo?

Keep the area clean, avoid direct sunlight, and apply the recommended ointment provided by our studio to ensure proper healing.

How does The Kala Culture ensure safety and hygiene?

We follow strict hygiene protocols, including sterilized single-use needles, disinfected equipment, and a clean, safe studio environment for every client.
