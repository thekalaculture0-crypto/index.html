Our Hygiene Standard

We don't just claim hygiene.
We own it.

A premium tattoo starts with a safe environment. Our hygiene routine is built into every appointment — from setup and sterilization to the final aftercare wrap.

◉

Single-Use Needles

Fresh, sterile, single-use needles are opened for every client and safely disposed of after the session.

✦

Sterile Equipment

Reusable equipment is handled through a strict cleaning and sterilization routine before it reaches your skin.

✚

Protected Workstation

High-touch surfaces and work areas are disinfected and prepared between clients.

◇

Fresh Gloves

Artists use fresh gloves and replace them whenever the workflow requires it to maintain a clean process.

▣

Clean, Professional Setup

Every appointment begins in a controlled, organized studio environment designed around safety and comfort.

✓

Aftercare Guidance

We finish with proper wrapping and clear aftercare guidance so your tattoo can heal the right way.

Hygiene isn't a promise we put on a poster. It's a standard we follow before, during and after every tattoo.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know before getting my first tattoo?

Discuss your design ideas with our expert artists, understand the process, and follow the aftercare instructions provided by our team.

What are the aftercare instructions for a new tattoo?

Keep the area clean, avoid direct sunlight, and apply the recommended ointment provided by our studio to ensure proper healing.

How does The Kala Culture ensure safety and hygiene?

We follow strict hygiene protocols, including sterilized single-use needles, disinfected equipment, and a clean, safe studio environment for every client.
